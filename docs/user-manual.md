# User Manual (Website Visitors & Buyers)

## Finding products

- **Browse**: Products menu → 13 categories → product pages with specs,
  applications, and sustainability notes
- **Search**: press **⌘K / Ctrl+K** anywhere, type what you need
  ("compostable coffee cups") — results are instant
- **Recommendation engine**: Tools → tell us sector + weight class

## Getting a quotation

1. Any product page → **Request quote online** (form pre-fills the product), or
2. **WhatsApp**: message the number in the footer; send `QUOTE P01 200`, or
3. **Telegram**: @SylvaraBot → `/quote P01 200`

Standard quotes arrive within one business hour as a PDF.

## Tracking an order

Reply `TRACK SO-2026-000123` on WhatsApp, `/track SO-…` on Telegram, or use
Support → Order tracking.

## Calculators (Tools page)

GSM/ream weight, box dimensions & board usage, carbon savings vs plastic,
and packaging recommendations — free, no login.

## Accessibility & preferences

Dark/light toggle in the header; full keyboard navigation; reduced-motion
respected automatically.
